# Vehicles

## Description

Vehicles are motorized and aerial assets utilized by security forces for deployment, rapid response, transportation, and tactical operations. They provide mobility for personnel, equipment transport, surveillance capabilities, and extended operational reach. Vehicles vary by deployment context (ground patrols, special response, aerial support) and are essential for multi-layered security coverage across terrain and airspace.

## Types

- **Ground - Standard Patrol** — General-purpose vehicles for routine patrols, area monitoring, rapid response, and personnel transport; equipped for visibility and deterrence with communications and emergency lighting.
- **Ground - Special Response Units (SRU)** — Tactical/armored vehicles designed for high-risk operations, riot response, breach operations, and rapid insertion of intervention teams; reinforced protection and specialized mounting systems.
- **Air - Rotary-wing (Helicopters)** — Manned aircraft for rapid deployment, reconnaissance, aerial support, casualty evacuation (CASEVAC), pursuit, and high-altitude observation; provides extended tactical mobility and real-time situational awareness.
- **Air - UAVs (Drones)** — Unmanned aerial vehicles for surveillance, reconnaissance, targeted monitoring, and intelligence gathering; low-risk persistent surveillance and real-time threat detection.

## Specifications by Vehicle Type

### Ground - Standard Patrol

**Vehicle Specifications:**

- **Seating Capacity**: 4-6 personnel (driver + crew + passengers)
- **Dimensions**: Mid-size sedan or SUV format
- **Engine**: Standard gasoline or diesel, 2.0L-3.0L
- **Performance**: 0-100 km/h in 8-12 seconds; top speed ~180 km/h
- **Fuel Tank**: 50-70 liters
- **Range**: 400-600 km on full tank
- **Curb Weight**: 1,500-1,800 kg
- **Payload Capacity**: 300-500 kg (personnel + equipment)

**Equipment:**

- Radio communication system (encrypted, multi-band)
- Roof-mounted emergency lights (LED, red/blue/white)
- Siren system (wail, yelp, air horn)
- Mobile data terminal (MDT) or tablet for dispatch/mapping
- GPS tracking and navigation system
- Dash camera and rear-view recording
- Equipment racks for gear storage
- First aid and trauma kit
- Portable barriers or cones (stored)
- Spotlight/searchlight
- Fuel jerry cans (auxiliary)

**Protective Features:**

- Standard body panels (no ballistic protection)
- Tempered/laminated windows
- Door locks with child safety enabled
- Basic armor package available (optional ballistic-resistant glass, door panels)
- Run-flat tire option (puncture-resistant)

**Maintenance Schedule:**

- Oil changes: Every 5,000 km or monthly
- Tire rotation: Every 10,000 km
- Filter replacement: Every 15,000 km
- Brake inspection: Every 20,000 km
- Full vehicle inspection: Every quarter

### Ground - Special Response Units (SRU)

**Vehicle Specifications:**

- **Seating Capacity**: 6-10 personnel (driver + gunner/commander + tactical team)
- **Dimensions**: Large SUV or armored platform
- **Engine**: Diesel, 3.0L-5.0L turbocharged
- **Performance**: 0-100 km/h in 12-18 seconds; top speed ~160 km/h (ballistic protection reduces speed)
- **Fuel Tank**: 80-120 liters
- **Range**: 500-800 km on full tank
- **Curb Weight**: 3,500-5,500 kg (includes armor plating)
- **Payload Capacity**: 1,000-1,500 kg (personnel + tactical equipment + weaponry)
- **Armor Rating**: Level B6/B7 (anti-ballistic, resistant to 7.62×51mm NATO rifle rounds)

**Equipment:**

- Encrypted tactical radio system (multiple channels, frequency hopping)
- Roof-mounted emergency lights (LED, programmable color sequences)
- Siren system (tactical, multiple tones)
- Tactical data interface (real-time team positioning, mission data)
- GPS/GNSS navigation and route planning
- Thermal imaging camera or FLIR system
- Multiple camera feeds (front, rear, sides, turret)
- Weapon mounting systems (flexible for rifles, machine guns, non-lethal launchers)
- Equipment racks for breach tools, shields, chemical protection gear
- Advanced first aid/trauma kit (tourniquet, hemostatic agents)
- Communication relay booster
- Portable power generator (auxiliary)
- Fuel transfer systems (on-scene refueling capability)

**Protective Features:**

- Ballistic-resistant body panels (Level B6/B7)
- Laminated ballistic glass (gun ports, observation windows)
- Reinforced steel frame and suspension
- Run-flat pneumatic tires with central tire inflation (CTIS)
- Mine-resistant undercarriage protection
- NBC (Nuclear, Biological, Chemical) filtration option
- Blast-resistant door hinges and seals
- Crew compartment separation with access hatch

**Maintenance Schedule:**

- Oil changes: Every 3,000 km or quarterly
- Tire pressure/CTIS check: Before each operation
- Filter replacement: Every 10,000 km
- Suspension inspection: Every 20,000 km
- Armor integrity audit: Every 6 months
- Full ballistic certification: Annually

### Air - Rotary-wing (Helicopters)

**Aircraft Specifications:**

- **Seating Capacity**: 4-8 personnel (pilot + co-pilot + crew + tactical team)
- **Dimensions**: Mid-size helicopter (e.g., UH-60 Blackhawk equivalent)
- **Engine**: Twin turbine, 1,500-2,000+ shaft horsepower per engine
- **Performance**: Cruise speed ~250 km/h; max speed ~300 km/h
- **Endurance**: 2.5-4 hours per flight (mission profile dependent)
- **Range**: 600-1,000 km (with reserves)
- **Operational Ceiling**: 5,500-6,000 meters
- **Payload**: 1,500-2,500 kg (personnel + equipment + fuel margin)

**Equipment:**

- Encrypted tactical radio system (aviation band + UHF/VHF comms)
- GPS/GNSS navigation and autopilot integration
- Search and Rescue (SAR) hoist system (rope, basket, or rescue litter)
- Night Vision Imaging System (NVIS) compatible lighting
- Thermal imaging camera (FLIR/infrared for threat detection)
- Electric or hydraulic-actuated door guns (50-caliber machine gun options)
- Rappel system with quick-release rigging
- Stretcher/litter mounts for CASEVAC operations
- Communication relay for ground units
- Cargo hook or external sling load attachment
- Emergency flotation gear
- Comprehensive avionics suite (altimeter, airspeed, attitude indicator, HSI)

**Protective Features:**

- Redundant hydraulic systems
- Engine fire suppression systems
- Ballistic self-sealing fuel tanks
- Crew armor (optional lightweight ballistic panels for pilots/crew)
- Terrain Awareness and Warning System (TAWS)
- Traffic Collision Avoidance System (TCAS)
- Emergency flotation system (water landing capability)
- Manual flight control reversion capability

**Maintenance Schedule:**

- Pre-flight inspection: Before each flight (~30 min)
- Daily inspection: After each flight (~1 hour)
- 25-hour inspection: Every 25 flight hours
- 100-hour inspection: Every 100 flight hours (major component checks)
- Transmission service: Every 500 flight hours
- Engine overhaul: Every 2,000-3,000 flight hours (manufacturer dependent)
- Avionics recertification: Annually

### Air - UAVs (Drones)

**Aircraft Specifications:**

**Tactical Reconnaissance UAV (Multi-rotor/Fixed-wing Hybrid):**

- **Seating**: N/A (unmanned, remote pilot + sensor operator)
- **Dimensions**: 0.5-1.5m wingspan/diameter (compact deployment)
- **Engine**: Electric (multi-rotor) or hybrid-electric (fixed-wing)
- **Performance**: Cruise speed 40-80 km/h; max speed 120+ km/h
- **Endurance**: 30 minutes to 2 hours (battery dependent; fixed-wing longer)
- **Operational Range**: 2-5 km line-of-sight (LOS); up to 50 km with relay/BVLOS systems
- **Operational Altitude**: 500-4,000 meters (airspace regulations permitting)
- **Payload Capacity**: 1-5 kg (camera, sensors, communication relay)

**Equipment:**

- High-resolution optical camera (4K/UHD)
- Thermal imaging camera (infrared, night capability)
- Zoom lens (20x optical, 50x+ digital)
- GPS/GNSS positioning and stabilization
- Obstacle avoidance sensors (LiDAR and/or optical)
- Encrypted communication link (secure data transmission)
- Real-time video downlink to ground station
- Signal intelligent (SIGINT) payload (frequency detection/analysis, optional)
- Laser range finder / target designator (optional)
- Recording storage (onboard SD card, cloud backup)
- Battery management system (hot-swap batteries)

**Protective Features:**

- Redundant GPS and IMU for flight stability
- Geofencing and no-fly zone restrictions (programmable)
- Return-to-home function (automatic landing if signal lost)
- Flight logging and black box data recorder
- Encryption of communication link and telemetry
- EMP-hardened control electronics (optional)
- Airframe protection (crash-resistant frame design)

**Maintenance Schedule:**

- Pre-flight battery check: Before each flight
- Motor inspection: Every 20 flight hours (wear, dust ingress)
- Propeller replacement: Every 10-15 flight hours (fatigue cracks, damage)
- Battery cycle capacity testing: Every 50 cycles
- Firmware updates: As released by manufacturer
- Sensor calibration: Monthly (optical and thermal drift correction)
- Full system inspection: Quarterly or after crashes/impacts

## Operational Deployment Notes

**Ground Vehicles:**

- Standard Patrol vehicles provide visible deterrence and rapid response within urban/suburban areas with road networks.
- SRU vehicles deploy for high-risk operations, riot control, barricaded suspect resolution, and dynamic entries—reserve asset, not for routine patrols.

**Air Assets:**

- Helicopters enable rapid deployment to remote/difficult-terrain locations, CASEVAC support during officer down scenarios, and extended surveillance.
- UAVs provide persistent low-cost surveillance, intelligence gathering, and minimal-risk threat assessment; ideal for extended monitoring of large areas.

**Integration:**

- Ground and air assets coordinate via encrypted communication systems to maximize coverage and response capability.
- Air surveillance feeds enable ground unit navigation, threat assessment, and route optimization in real-time.
