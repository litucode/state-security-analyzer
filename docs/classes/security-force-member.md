# Security Force Member

## Description

A security force member is a trained personnel responsible for protecting people, property, infrastructure, and assets. They maintain order, enforce rules/laws, deter threats, respond to incidents, and provide rapid intervention when needed. Roles vary by context (public police, private security, military police, transit authorities, etc.), but typically involve patrolling, access control, threat response, and sometimes specialized high-risk operations.

## Types

- **Transit** — Focused on public transportation systems (buses, trains, subways, airports); duties include fare enforcement, crowd control, passenger safety, and countering theft/vandalism/terrorism in transit environments.
- **Patrol** — General/mobile security; routine area monitoring, visible deterrence, responding to alarms/calls, traffic/pedestrian control, and preventive presence.
- **Intervention** — Active response teams; handle escalating incidents, de-escalation, arrests, crowd management, or support during disturbances/medical emergencies.
- **Special Intervention** — Elite/high-risk units (e.g., SWAT-style, counter-terrorism, hostage rescue, or tactical response); execute dynamic entries, barricaded suspect resolution, high-threat arrests, VIP protection, or specialized operations requiring advanced tactics and gear.

## Equipment by Role Type

Equipment varies significantly by role type, threat level, and jurisdiction. Below are typical equipment profiles for each security force member type:

### Transit Agent Equipment

**Personal Equipment:**

- Duty belt (for quick access to tools)
- Handcuffs and restraints
- Radio/communication device (two-way portable radio)
- Flashlight (high-lumen)
- Baton or impact weapon
- Pepper spray / OC spray
- Body camera
- Notebook, pen, and ID/badge
- Medical/first aid kit (basic trauma items)
- High-visibility vest (for safety and deterrence)

**Personal Protective Equipment (PEE):**

- Basic helmet (impact-resistant)
- Soft body armor vest (stab-resistant)
- Tactical gloves (basic protection)
- Safety goggles
- Boots (slip-resistant)
- High-visibility outer garment (rain/windproof, reflective)

### Patrol Officer Equipment

**Personal Equipment:**

- Duty belt (for quick access to tools)
- Handcuffs and restraints
- Radio/communication device (two-way portable radio or body-worn comms)
- Flashlight (tactical, high-lumen)
- Baton or impact weapon
- Pepper spray / OC spray
- Taser / conducted energy weapon (where authorized)
- Firearm / sidearm (for armed roles) + extra magazines
- Holster and magazine pouches
- Body camera
- Notebook, pen, and ID/badge
- Medical/first aid kit (basic trauma items)
- Multi-tool

**Personal Protective Equipment (PEE):**

- Tactical helmet (ballistic-resistant, Level IIIA)
- Bullet-proof vest / body armor (Level II/IIIA)
- Tactical gloves (cut-resistant, padded)
- Ballistic eyewear / safety goggles
- Knee & elbow pads
- Boots (tactical, steel-toed, slip-resistant)
- High-visibility vest (for traffic control)

### Intervention Team Equipment

**Personal Equipment:**

- Duty belt (for quick access to tools)
- Handcuffs and restraints
- Radio/communication device (two-way portable radio or body-worn comms)
- Flashlight (tactical, high-lumen)
- Baton or impact weapon
- Pepper spray / OC spray
- Taser / conducted energy weapon
- Firearm / sidearm + extra magazines
- Holster and magazine pouches
- Body camera
- Notebook, pen, and ID/badge
- Medical/first aid kit (trauma items including tourniquet)
- Multi-tool

**Personal Protective Equipment (PEE):**

- Helmet (ballistic/tactical, Level IIIA)
- Bullet-proof vest / body armor (Level III)
- Ballistic plates / inserts (for enhanced protection)
- Tactical gloves (cut-resistant, reinforced)
- Ballistic eyewear
- Knee & elbow pads
- Boots (tactical, steel-toed)
- Neck protector / collar (ballistic-resistant)
- High-visibility outer garment (when needed)

### Special Intervention Equipment

**Personal Equipment:**

- Duty belt (for quick access to tools)
- Handcuffs and restraints
- Radio/communication device (encrypted, body-worn comms)
- Flashlight (tactical, high-lumen)
- Baton or impact weapon
- Pepper spray / OC spray
- Taser / conducted energy weapon
- Firearm / sidearm + extra magazines
- Holster and magazine pouches
- Body camera
- Notebook, pen, and ID/badge
- Medical/first aid kit (advanced trauma items)
- Multi-tool or knife
- Breaching tools (for dynamic entries)

**Personal Protective Equipment (PEE):**

- Helmet (ballistic/tactical, Level III or higher)
- Bullet-proof vest / body armor (Level III/IV)
- Ballistic plates / inserts (ceramic or steel for rifle protection)
- Tactical gloves (cut-resistant, padded, reinforced)
- Ballistic eyewear
- Knee & elbow pads
- Boots (tactical, steel-toed, slip-resistant)
- Neck protector / collar (ballistic or stab-resistant)
- Groin/throat protectors
- High-visibility outer garment (when needed for operations)
